# One Word Wiser review checks

Review the exact final body, with source notes available separately. Record pass, fail or pending for each applicable check, with a specific example or source. A claim that something was checked is not evidence of a fresh check. Reuse a dated source review when the relevant claims are unchanged and identify it as reused. Edits invalidate affected checks; identify the reviewed artifact by commit or hash.

1. **Title payoff:** A clear title names a recognizable concern or useful discovery, and the body delivers. No inflated or invented promise.
2. **Hebrew fidelity:** Niqqud, word form, transliteration and meaning match checked sources. Literal meaning and later commentary remain distinct; no forced mistranslation or decorative letter symbolism presented as fact.
3. **Source fidelity:** Every factual teaching and quotation has a verified source in context. Attribution is accurate. Evan's application is identifiable as interpretation. No invented personal or pastoral story.
4. **Useful free teaching:** A new reader learns something complete without Hebrew background. Any paywall follows the current dated policy and approved benefit. No unavailable resource is promised.
5. **Human reflection:** One concrete need connects naturally to the word, with a specific practice, question or insight. The writing does not merely repeat a slogan or prescribe a feeling.
6. **Voice and respect:** Plain, warm and restrained; no banned language, melodrama, universal claims about traditions or condescension toward Christian readers. Gospel echoes are relevant, sourced and within the week's limit.
7. **Reading experience:** Short paragraphs, meaningful subheadings, clear Hebrew, restrained divider, coherent ending and sign-off. Check the actual saved Substack phone preview before claiming mobile verification; source structure alone earns only a structural check.
8. **Packaging:** Correct subject, subtitle, audience, paywall and authorized assets. Header/source notes/proposed Notes excluded from the body. Links and any Hebrew in rendered assets inspected. Preserve approved copy during upload.
9. **Approval and state:** Evan's approval and the authorized next action are explicitly recorded. A planned date is not authorization. Check for an existing draft before creating one. Report actual draft/scheduled/published state with evidence.

Revise failures, then re-evaluate the affected checks. Stop after two revision passes to present any unresolved issue with a recommendation. Pending human approval, external evidence or preview stays pending; do not revise prose endlessly to manufacture a pass.

Use the operating system's cycle template for a full experiment; a brief checklist alongside the post is sufficient for ordinary work. Keep learned reusable guidance in `learning.md`; keep run history under `one-word-wiser/operations/cycles/`.
