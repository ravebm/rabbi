## Recommended format

Keep the article readable and preserve its human title. Restore a prominent Hebrew-word image for the post's social preview and prepare one companion Note around the same teaching. Social-preview artwork can be uploaded separately from the article body; removing an image from the letter need not remove it from the feed.

Use the existing cream, navy and gold word-card design as the starting point. Hebrew with correct vowel marks, transliteration, one English meaning, restrained publication credit. Large legible letters; avoid a paragraph of small type. Retire the old Morning/Evening label and date from future social cards unless they serve the specific use. Do not change existing published thumbnails from this plan alone. Inspect the actual feed crop before reporting a successful update.

Substack recommends at least 1200 × 630 for social-preview images. Existing word cards are 1200 × 675; assess the crop and keep all essential text away from the edges. No new image was rendered in this review.

## A small 14-day experiment

1. Prepare one original Note per weekday using the current letter: a 40–90-word teaching useful by itself, a clear Hebrew-word card, and one restrained route to the full free letter. This is a proposed cadence, not a scheduler or publishing authorization.
2. Give a real interpretive insight rather than a generic quote or a notice saying a new article is out. Alternate a Hebrew detail, a human scene, or a good textual question. Avoid suggesting the Bible mistranslates a word to manufacture surprise.
3. Spend about 15 minutes on three days each week reading and drafting thoughtful replies in relevant conversations. Only post comments with current authorization. No automated flattery, unrelated self-promotion, copied replies, or invented reading.
4. Identify 8–12 compatible publications: thoughtful Christian Bible readers and teachers, Jewish interpretation, contemplative faith, and interfaith conversation. Read enough work to establish fit; prepare two specific collaboration/recommendation proposals. Do not recommend automatically or contact writers without Evan's authorization. Recommendations are editorial endorsements, not reciprocal transactions.
5. Make the free subscription promise explicit at the profile and relevant invitation: one Hebrew word each morning that opens a biblical passage and a question about life, no Hebrew required. Profile editing is proposed, not performed. The current profile leads with a broad rabbinic biography rather than this concrete daily benefit.
6. Let the complete free letter build trust. Present The Rabbi's Notes as the next step for readers who want close study. Avoid making a paid upgrade the first demand of every discovery Note.

## Evaluate

Record each Note's URL, image treatment, topic, publication time, impressions/views, free subscriptions, paid starts, follows, and production effort where available. Compare at 72 hours and seven days. Missing attribution is unavailable, not zero. Separate follows from email subscriptions. Use the aggregate Growth sources page alongside Note-level View stats.

The first decision is whether the word-card/short-teaching combination brings interested subscribers at a sustainable level of effort. Likes and restacks support diagnosis but do not establish conversion. Fourteen days is an initial learning period; small counts and different subjects do not support strong causal claims. No target subscriber count or guaranteed conversion lift is asserted.

## Primary documentation checked

- [Notes and Note-level stats](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/14564821756308-Getting-started-on-Substack-Notes): Home feed includes network activity and restacks; View stats includes free/paid subscribers, follows, clicks and shares, available within 24 hours and usually refreshed hourly.
- [Recommendations](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/5036794583828-How-can-I-recommend-other-publications-on-Substack): appears in subscription flow, homepage and recommendation digests; dashboard reports generated free subscriptions. Adding recommendations can notify other writers and readers.
- [Social preview](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039016992-How-do-I-edit-what-my-post-looks-like-on-social-media): Settings → social preview → Upload New; recommended minimum image dimensions 1200 × 630.
- [Following versus subscribing](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/18261513315348-How-does-following-work-on-Substack): following exposes Notes/activity; a subscription delivers publication posts through email/app.
