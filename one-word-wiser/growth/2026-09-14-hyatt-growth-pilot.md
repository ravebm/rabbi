### Draft recording bullets

Working title: You Can Still Come Home.

- Have you ever put off an apology because you couldn't find the right words?
- A Hebrew word I return to at this time of year is teshuvah. It means return, and it is the Jewish word for repentance.
- That image gives us something practical to do with regret: turn toward the person we've hurt, the responsibility we've avoided, or the God we've stopped speaking to.
- Returning doesn't undo what happened. It gives us a way to begin making things right.
- Perhaps the first sentence is simply: “I'm sorry. Can we talk?”
- I write about Hebrew and Jewish wisdom in One Word Wiser. You can read the full reflection, “You Can Still Come Home,” at rabbi.substack.com.

These are prompts for Evan's natural delivery, not a demand for exact recital. Aim for about one minute; measure actual delivery. Pause between thoughts and before retakes as Ray describes. No recording or edited video exists yet.
