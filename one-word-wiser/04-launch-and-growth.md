## Growth experiments, not commitments

The [September 14 Hyatt growth pilot](growth/2026-09-14-hyatt-growth-pilot.md) turns one existing teaching into recording bullets, a Note, a video caption, and a production brief. It records the agent ownership Evan requested and a proposed first-week experiment. The copy is prepared for review; media and distribution have not run.

- Test clear human titles tied to a real question, while keeping the Hebrew visible in the letter. Do not change titles based on same-day opens alone.
- Invite readers to share a useful word with one person. Prepare Notes from good lines when requested; do not assume two automatic Notes a day are running or required.
- Consider a restrained Already Home cross-mention and appropriate recommendations. Contacting another writer or emailing another list requires explicit authorization.
- Consider the existing Facebook creative only after the landing page and offer are consistent. Set a user-approved budget and measure ad-cohort free acquisition, later paid starts, and retention. No automatic spend or scale rule.
- For the study-companion proposal, learn whether readers actually use the page alone or with a group. Do not promise group discounts or church licensing without a decision.

## Measurement

Track net free growth, cancellations, mature post-level paid starts, retention, and specific reader feedback. Compare like observation windows and separate paid and free cohorts. Opens and downloads are supporting signals, not revenue or causality.

The public Hebrew for Christians offer includes study materials and rabbinic context. Its subscriber count is not evidence of its paid conversion. Already Home supplies useful editorial hypotheses, not a transferable conversion guarantee. Do not claim Substack’s algorithm rewards Hebrew script without evidence.
