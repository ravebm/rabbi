# One Word Wiser

**Publication:** rabbi.substack.com<br>
**Author:** Rabbi Evan Moffic<br>
**Promise:** One Hebrew word, one rabbinic teaching, and one wiser way to live.

A daily letter about Hebrew, the Bible, and Jewish wisdom. Christians are the main growth audience; Jewish readers and anyone curious are welcome. The reader should learn something useful and hear a rabbi address a recognizable human question.

## Current operating shape

| Element | Current direction |
|---|---|
| Cadence | One morning email at 6:00 America/Chicago, seven days a week |
| Weekdays | A complete free teaching, followed in the same email by a fuller rabbinic reflection |
| Saturday | A word from the week’s Torah reading; the whole letter is free |
| Sunday | A reader’s verse; the whole letter is free |
| Paid | Full weekday reflections. Existing prices: $7/month, $60/year, $180 founding |
| Launch | All letters free in full through September 27, 2026; existing plan places weekday gates from September 28, subject to Evan’s decision during this rethink |
| Presentation | Clear human title, visible Hebrew, short paragraphs, a few useful subheadings, restrained dividers |
| Word selection | Familiar passage, accurate Hebrew, a useful teaching, a grounded rabbinic reading, pronounceable word |

Evan’s September 14 correction retires “secret” as the paid product and “Let It Change Your Life” as a heading. The familiar format should leave room for real discovery, without a mandatory hidden meaning, dramatic claim, or identical moral every day.

## Proposal under review

**The Rabbi’s Notes:** one weekly, one-page companion to a passage already being taught. Hebrew, context, two short rabbinic observations, and two questions for personal or group study. See `study-companion/README.md` and its sample. This is not promised on Substack and does not replace existing paid benefits until Evan decides.
