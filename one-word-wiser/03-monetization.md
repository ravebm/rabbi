## What to learn

Already Home’s historical results suggest that a recognizable human concern and a useful response are worth testing here. They do not establish a conversion rate for a different audience. Hebrew for Christians publicly offers study materials and rabbinic context; its public audience size is not paid revenue evidence.

Use current Substack data, with dates and mature observation windows: net free growth, post-attributed paid starts, cancellations, and reader replies about actual use. For a study-companion pilot, track whether people read it, print it, or use it with a group, alongside paid starts and retention. Downloads alone do not prove paid demand.

The old subscriber/revenue forecast has been retired because it used an unverified starting list and transferred Already Home’s conversion rate. Do not promise a revenue result. Choose a pilot decision after a few real examples and reader response.
