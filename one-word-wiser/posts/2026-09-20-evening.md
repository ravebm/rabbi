# Sunday evening — 🌙 LAILA TOV · *kippur*
**Subject:** 🌅 The week ahead: Atonement
**Section:** Morning · **Paywall:** none · **Send:** 7:00 pm CT
**Notes:** (1) An honest beginning starts with what actually happened. for 9 am  (2) There is a next page, even when you cannot erase the last one. for 9 pm

---

*Yom Kippur*, the Day of Atonement, begins tonight and continues through Monday. Our week begins with its question: how do we begin again with what we have actually done?

You may spend tomorrow in prayer. You may spend it at work. The question has a place in either day.

Five words will keep us company:

- **Monday: כִּפּוּר · *kippur*, atonement.** Leviticus gives us a day for cleansing, and the rabbis ask what still needs mending between people.
- **Tuesday: וִדּוּי · *vidui*, confession.** We will consider what changes when the wrong we have been explaining becomes something we can name.
- **Wednesday: צוֹם · *tzom*, fast.** Isaiah asks whether our fasting helps someone else go free.
- **Thursday: יוֹנָה · *yonah*, dove, and the name Jonah.** We will meet a prophet who has trouble accepting mercy for people he dislikes.
- **Friday: נֶפֶשׁ · *nefesh*, soul or living being.** As *Sukkot*, the festival of shelters, begins that evening, Genesis will bring us back to the life God breathes into a body.

There is a next page, even when you cannot erase the last one.

Monday morning, 6:00. Say it once tonight: *kippur*, atonement. Kee-POOR.

See you at sunrise.

— Rabbi Evan
