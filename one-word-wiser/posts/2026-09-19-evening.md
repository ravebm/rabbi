# Saturday evening — 🌙 LAILA TOV · *havdalah*
**Subject:** 🕯️ The week in five words: The Days of Awe
**Section:** Morning · **Paywall:** none · **Send:** 7:00 pm CT
**Notes:** (1) Love keeps its promises in small, ordinary ways. for 9 am  (2) Let someone else's name rest beside yours. for 9 pm

---

These Days of Awe have given us five words for beginning again.

- [תְּשׁוּבָה · *teshuvah* · return](MORNING_POST_URL): You can turn toward home.
- [שְׁמַע · *shema* · hear](MORNING_POST_URL): Leave room for another person's answer.
- [סְלִיחָה · *selichah* · forgiveness](MORNING_POST_URL): You can tell the truth before God.
- [חֶסֶד · *chesed* · loyal love](MORNING_POST_URL): Care continues after the first impulse passes.
- [חַיִּים · *chayim* · life](MORNING_POST_URL): Your choices reach beyond you.

Five lines to carry, Monday through Friday:

- *Repentance is a feeling. Return is a road.*
- *Listening leaves room for an answer you had not prepared.*
- *Forgiveness gives us somewhere to tell the truth.*
- *Love keeps its promises in small, ordinary ways.*
- *Choose what gives life a little more room.*

Five lines to sleep on, in the same order:

- *You don't have to walk the whole road tonight. You only have to turn.*
- *Let the part of you that wants to win help you listen longer.*
- *You can remember what happened and still let something new happen.*
- *Your kindness matters even when no thank-you comes back.*
- *Let someone else's name rest beside yours.*

*If one of these words opened something for you this week, tell one person. A note from you reaches someone no ad ever could.*

*Shavua tov* — a good week.

— Rabbi Evan
